Meshtastic settings

The radio plan for this county mesh

These are the settings that let your node hear (and be heard by) the rest of OconeeConnect. Flash steps are on the Connect page.

Region US · preset LongFast · frequency slot 20 · hop limit 3 · handheld role CLIENT.

How a message hops

  1. Each person has a node.
  2. Your phone connects over Bluetooth.
  3. A text goes out over 915 MHz LoRa.
  4. Nearby Meshtastic nodes on the same channel rebroadcast it, up to the hop limit.
  5. Location can ride along if the board has GPS and the channel allows it.

Channels

  • The primary channel stays the public LongFast channel until a household QR is published. The default key is public, so don’t put secrets on it.
  • You can add a secondary channel with a private key for family or a work crew.
  • South Carolina Mesh publishes an optional secondary named SCMesh for the wider state map. Check their current join page at scmesh.us if you want that. It’s not required to hear local Oconee nodes.
  • Don’t rename the primary channel and leave the frequency slot at 0. The slot is derived from the name. Set it to 20 explicitly.

MQTT and position

MQTT is optional and is not the mesh itself. If you uplink, leave encryption enabled and coarsen your position. Map reports are unencrypted by design. A node sitting in a drawer doesn’t need a precise map pin.

If you still have MeshCore on a board

Just reflash Meshtastic (and erase first). MeshCore won’t exchange messages with this mesh. Official docs: meshtastic.org.
