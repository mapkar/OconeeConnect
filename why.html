Why mesh radio here

Oconee already knows what silence feels like

Cell towers fail. Generators run out. Valleys swallow signal on a clear day. A local mesh does not replace 911 or a phone plan. It keeps a short text moving when the usual path is dark.

Open and cheap

  • Hardware is usually $30–50.
  • No contract and no monthly fee after you own the radio.
  • No company has to approve your node.

Hurricane Helene — September 2024

The Upstate took heavy rain, flooding, and long power outages. Cell sites died when backup power ended or hardware got wet. Battery and solar nodes do not wait on a diesel truck.

January 2026 Verizon outage

A nationwide carrier failure left phones on SOS. In-county neighbors still needed to say “I’m okay” and “stay put.” That is the job of a local radio mesh.

Everyday Oconee

Lake Keowee, Jocassee, the Foothills Trail, Sumter National Forest, and the gaps between Westminster and Mountain Rest are exactly where a $40 radio earns its keep — check-ins, locations, “running late,” “road closed.”
