This county

Lakes, trails, towns, and dead zones

Oconee is South Carolina’s western corner — Blue Ridge foothills against Georgia and North Carolina. About 82,000 people. Small towns, a lot of trees, and water in every direction.

Towns

  • Seneca — largest community. First hub.
  • Walhalla — county seat. Second hub.
  • Westminster, Salem, West Union — the rural pockets where a single high node changes everything.

Why the radio has a hard job

  • Keowee, Jocassee, Hartwell, Tugaloo — water and ridges.
  • Chattooga and Chauga drainages.
  • Foothills Trail, Palmetto Trail, Sumter National Forest.
  • Cell that looks fine on a highway and vanishes a mile off it.

That geography is the whole argument for planned repeaters instead of hoping pocket nodes find each other. Read the coverage plan.
