OCONEECONNECT

Off-grid mesh communication for Oconee County, SC

Stay in touch across Seneca, Walhalla, Lake Keowee, and the Blue Ridge foothills when cell service is weak, towers are down, or you are simply off the pavement.

OconeeConnect is a community LoRa mesh. It is local-first: short texts, locations, and alerts that hop radio-to-radio. No monthly plan. No account. No internet required for the radios themselves.

Planned backbone protocol: MeshCore on the US 915 MHz band. Dedicated solar repeaters first in Seneca and Walhalla, bridged near Poplar Springs Road and Blue Ridge Boulevard. Meshtastic is widely used here too — same radios, different firmware. The two do not talk to each other over the air. See Mesh and Meshtastic.

What this is not

  • Not a cell replacement for voice or 911.
  • Not finished county-wide coverage. The splash is honest: rollout is early.
  • Not a commercial network. Sites are volunteered. Hardware is yours.
