OCONEECONNECT

A community mesh for Oconee County, SC

Stay connected with neighbors across Seneca, Walhalla, Lake Keowee, and the Blue Ridge foothills — even when cell service is weak, towers are down, or you’re off the pavement.

OconeeConnect is a local-first Meshtastic mesh. Radios hop short texts, locations, and simple alerts from one to the next. No monthly plan. No account. No internet required for the radios themselves.

We run Meshtastic on the US 915 MHz band: region US, LongFast preset, hop limit 3, frequency slot 20. The first solar routers will go up in Seneca and Walhalla, linked near Poplar Springs Road and Blue Ridge Boulevard. Handhelds stay in CLIENT role so they don’t crowd the network. See Mesh and Settings for the details.

What this is not

  • Not a replacement for cell service, voice calls, or 911.
  • Not finished county-wide coverage yet. We’re still early — the splash page is honest about that.
  • Not a commercial network. Sites are volunteered and hardware is yours to buy and own.
  • Not MeshCore. The boards can run either firmware, but they don’t talk to each other. If a node is still on MeshCore, just reflash it to Meshtastic.

Live maps of whatever is already on the air: MeshMap.net. Statewide Meshtastic talk: South Carolina Mesh. Public contact: @0k_sashes on X.
