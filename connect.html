How to connect

Join the OconeeConnect Meshtastic mesh

About 15–30 minutes if the USB cable is a real data cable. Channel and role details: Settings.

  1. Get a 915 MHz board. Heltec V3/V4, RAK4631 / RAK19003, T-Beam, Seeed trackers. Confirm the listing says US / 915 MHz.
  2. Attach the antenna first. Then power the board.
  3. Flash Meshtastic. On a computer, open Chrome or Edge (not Safari/Firefox). Go to flasher.meshtastic.org. USB in, pick your board, flash the latest stable. If the board previously ran MeshCore or an old Meshtastic build, do a full erase on the first flash.
  4. Install the Meshtastic app. iOS App Store or Android Play Store. Browser client: client.meshtastic.org.
  5. Set the radio. Pair over Bluetooth. LoRa region US. Preset LongFast. Set the frequency slot to 20 instead of leaving it at 0, so a renamed channel cannot move you off the public frequency. Hop limit 3. Role CLIENT unless this box is an agreed solar site. If you cannot hear anyone, region, slot, or firmware does not match — ask on X @0k_sashes before you guess.
  6. Name the node. Something that says who and where you are (town helps). Send a short test on the primary channel once another node is in range.

If nothing appears

  • Wrong region, slot, or firmware. Most “dead radio” problems are this.
  • Charge-only USB cable. The flasher never sees the board.
  • You are in a hole and no router hears you yet. That is a coverage problem, not a broken radio. See Coverage.
  • You flashed MeshCore by habit. Reflash Meshtastic. The two do not talk.
A private household channel will be published as a QR once the first two hubs are on the air and stable. Until then, LongFast slot 20 is the public landing. Do not treat a random secondary key as “the Oconee channel.”
