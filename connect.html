How to connect

Join the OconeeConnect MeshCore mesh

Most people are on the air in 15–30 minutes once they have a real data USB cable. Detailed radio and role notes are on the Settings page.

  1. Get a 915 MHz board. Heltec V3/V4, RAK4631 / RAK19003, T-Beam, or Seeed trackers all work. Just confirm the listing says US / 915 MHz.
  2. Attach the antenna first. Then power the board. (Always antenna before power.)
  3. Flash MeshCore. On a computer, open Chrome or Edge (Safari and Firefox usually won’t work for flashing). Go to flasher.meshcore.io. Plug in via USB, pick your board, choose Companion (for handhelds) or Repeater (for fixed sites), and flash the latest stable build. If the board previously ran Meshtastic or older firmware, choose the erase option on the first flash.
  4. Install the MeshCore app. iOS App Store or Android Play Store. Search for MeshCore.
  5. Set the radio. Pair over Bluetooth. Select the USA/Canada (Recommended) preset. This sets frequency to 910.525 MHz, bandwidth 62.5 kHz, SF 7, coding rate 5. For handhelds stay on Companion firmware. If you don’t hear anyone right away, double-check the preset and firmware — or ask on X @0k_sashes before changing other settings.
  6. Name the node. Something that says who and roughly where you are (town helps). Send a short test on the public channel once another node is in range.

If nothing appears

  • Wrong preset, wrong firmware, or not yet in range of a repeater. This is the most common reason a radio seems silent.
  • Charge-only USB cable. The flasher never sees the board.
  • You’re in a low spot and no repeater hears you yet. That’s a coverage question, not a broken radio. See the Coverage page.
  • The board is still running Meshtastic. Just reflash MeshCore — the two firmwares don’t exchange messages.
A private household channel will be published once the first two hubs are stable on the air. Until then, the public channel on the USA/Canada preset is the place to meet.
