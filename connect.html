How to connect

Join the OconeeConnect MeshCore mesh

About 15–30 minutes if the USB cable is a real data cable. Want Meshtastic instead? Use the Meshtastic guide.

  1. Get a 915 MHz board. Heltec V3/V4, RAK4631 / RAK19003, T-Beam, Seeed trackers. Confirm the listing says US / 915 MHz.
  2. Attach the antenna first. Then power the board.
  3. Flash MeshCore. On a computer, open Chrome or Edge (not Safari/Firefox). Go to flasher.meshcore.co.uk. USB in, pick your board, pick the role: Companion for a personal radio, Repeater only if this box will live on a roof. If the board previously ran Meshtastic, do a full erase on the first MeshCore flash.
  4. Install the MeshCore app. iOS App Store or Android Play Store. App list: meshcore.co.uk/apps.html.
  5. Set the radio preset. Pair over Bluetooth. Choose the USA/Canada region/preset so you are in the 902–928 MHz plan. OconeeConnect’s first repeaters will start at a higher spreading factor (SF11) for range, then drop toward SF5 as the map fills in. If you cannot hear anyone, the spreading factor or frequency does not match the local backbone — ask on X @0k_sashes or a local group for the current numbers before you guess.
  6. Name the node. Something that says who and where you are (town helps). Send a short test into the public room once a repeater is in range.

If nothing appears

  • Wrong region or SF. Most “dead radio” problems are this.
  • Charge-only USB cable. The flasher never sees the board.
  • You are in a hole and no repeater hears you yet. That is a coverage problem, not a broken radio. See Coverage.
  • You flashed Meshtastic by habit. Reflash MeshCore, or stay on Meshtastic and use that mesh instead.
We will publish a channel QR and the locked radio numbers on this page once the first two hubs are on the air and stable. Until then, do not treat a random default as “the Oconee channel.”
