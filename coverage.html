Coverage plan

Decentralized radios. Planned backbone.

Oconee is wide, folded, and wet. Mountains, valleys, hills, and lakes block line of sight. A mesh only works here if someone puts repeaters on purpose.

Phase 1 — Seneca and Walhalla

First goal: reliable coverage in the two largest population centers, then a link between them.

  • Central repeater coverage for Seneca.
  • Central repeater coverage for Walhalla.
  • A backbone relay near Poplar Springs Road and Blue Ridge Boulevard (US 28) to bridge the two.

Later — height

A high-performance remote repeater on a mountainside, once property is identified, to open the rest of the county so neighborhood nodes can attach to something that already hears both towns.
