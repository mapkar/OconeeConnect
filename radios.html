Radios & repeaters

What to buy · what we mount

Buy US 915 MHz hardware only. EU 868 MHz boards will not work here. Never key the radio without an antenna attached.

Handheld / companion (start here)

These talk to your phone and send your messages into the mesh. Typical cost $25–50.

  • Heltec V3 / V4 or Wireless Tracker — common, cheap, easy to flash.
  • LilyGO T-Beam or T-Deck — GPS on the T-Beam; screen/keys on the T-Deck.
  • Seeed Xiao / Wio Tracker / T1000-E — small boards, good for packs and pockets.

Upgrade the stock antenna to a real 915 MHz whip when you can. Hills eat weak antennas.

Fixed repeaters (the backbone)

MeshCore coverage comes from dedicated repeaters, not from every phone in a pocket. First-wave design for OconeeConnect:

  • Base: RAK19003 (WisBlock mini) class solar repeaters.
  • Omnidirectional 915 MHz antenna.
  • Mounts that fit the site: magnet, pole clamp, or mast.
  • Power: at least a 3.7 V 5,000 mAh LiPo plus enough solar to keep it up through winter overcast.

Where a ready-made enclosure is easier (billboard, school pole, or a temporary flagpole), a packaged unit such as the Seeed SenseCAP Solar Node P1-class is the kind of box site owners can understand: small, solar, no grid drop.

What a good site looks like

  • Height and a clean view toward Seneca, Walhalla, or both.
  • Water towers, masts, tall buildings, hilltops, light or flag poles, mountainside property.
  • Access for a yearly battery/antenna check.
  • No need for building power if solar is honest about shade.
